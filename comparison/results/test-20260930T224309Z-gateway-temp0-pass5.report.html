How lopsided is 14 to 9? If the two models were equally good, which one gets each of the 23 split messages right would be a coin flip. Flip 23 coins, and a split of 14 to 9 or more lopsided, either way, comes up about 40 times in 100 (this number is called the p-value: p = 0.4049). The usual bar for calling a difference clear is below 5 in 100. That is above the bar, so this sample does not show a clear difference. It does not prove the two are equally good; more messages might show one. The name of this test, if you want to look it up: exact McNemar's test.
